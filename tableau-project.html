TABLEAU • DATA ANALYTICS

Customer Lifecycle Management Analytics

A data analytics project focused on understanding customer lifecycle stages, behavior, engagement, churn, and reactivation through interactive Tableau dashboards.

Project Overview

This project involved analyzing customer lifecycle behavior and creating dashboards to understand how customers move through different stages of their journey.

The analysis focused on identifying customer patterns, important performance indicators, drop-off points, churn behavior, and opportunities for re-engagement.

Key Areas Analyzed

  • Customer lifecycle stages
  • KPI performance
  • Customer funnel analysis
  • Churn and reactivation patterns
  • Cohort analysis
  • RFM segmentation
  • Year-over-year analysis
  • Customer behavior and engagement

Tools & Skills

  • Tableau
  • Data Analysis
  • Data Visualization
  • KPI Analysis
  • Customer Segmentation
  • Dashboard Development

What I Worked On

I worked on developing and analyzing Tableau dashboards to present customer lifecycle insights in a clear and structured way. The dashboards included KPI cards, funnel analysis, cohort analysis, churn and reactivation analysis, and RFM segmentation.

This project helped me strengthen my practical understanding of data visualization, customer analytics, dashboard design, and communicating data-driven insights.

Project Outcome

The analysis provided a structured view of customer behavior across different lifecycle stages and helped identify areas that can be explored for improving customer engagement and retention.
